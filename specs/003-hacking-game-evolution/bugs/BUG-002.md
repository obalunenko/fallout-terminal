# Bug Report: Force-Solved Hack Shows a Stale Invalid-Action Rejection

**Type**: Untested flow
**Severity**: Medium
**Feature**: `feature/003-hacking-game-evolution` — Phase 1 Server-Authoritative Hacking Patterns
**Reported**: 2026-08-28
**Status**: Patched
**Patched**: 2026-08-28
**Implemented**: 2026-08-28
**Verified**: 2026-08-28

## Description

After a player submits an unsuccessful hacking guess, the Overseer can use the private
`ForceHackSuccess` control to complete the still-active puzzle. The authoritative hack state reaches
success, but players can then see the ambiguous raw notice
`ДЕЙСТВИЕ ОТКЛОНЕНО: invalid-action`. The notice is visible on the hacking surface and can remain
visible after the success transition returns the player to the terminal menu.

The player-facing result therefore contradicts the successful Overseer action: the puzzle is solved,
but the most prominent status says that an unspecified action was rejected. Players cannot tell
whether the hack succeeded, which action was rejected, or whether they need to act again.

**Observed reproduction**:

1. Enter an active hacking puzzle as a player.
2. Submit an incorrect candidate-word guess.
3. While the puzzle remains eligible, invoke the Overseer `ForceHackSuccess` control.
4. Observe the authoritative success transition and the player notice on the hacking surface and
   subsequent terminal menu.

**Expected**: Every player follows one unambiguous shared success flow. A late result belonging to
the superseded hacking interaction does not replace the solved outcome with a raw rejection or remain
visible after the hacking context ends.

**Actual**: A late or concurrently reconciled action result can publish the generic
`invalid-action` rejection after the solved snapshot, and the transient notice persists across the
hacking-to-menu transition.

**Evidence**: `/Users/olegbalunenko/Desktop/Screenshot 2026-08-28 at 03.41.42.png` shows the solved
flow returned to the terminal list while `ДЕЙСТВИЕ ОТКЛОНЕНО: invalid-action` remains visible.

## Root Cause Analysis

The authoritative gameplay mutation and the player-facing action-result channel are individually
ordered, but the browser does not define precedence between a terminal hack snapshot and a later
result associated with the former hacking context. When the solved snapshot arrives, the player enters
the success flow, but pending shared-action or presentation bookkeeping and its transient notice are
not comprehensively invalidated by the solved generation. A later rejected result is therefore treated
as a new user-visible failure and rendered as the raw reason `invalid-action`.

The notice has no hacking-generation or presentation-context lifetime. Once written, it remains the
fallback player notice until another path explicitly clears it, so the message can survive the delayed
success transition from the hacking screen to the terminal menu. Existing tests verify forced success,
ordinary rejection, and presentation reconciliation in isolation; none exercise this reported ordering.
