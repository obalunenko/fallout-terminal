## Root Cause Analysis

The prior closure split the behavior into two controlled cases: a partial candidate is correctly rejected for its independently split edge, while an explicitly created all-three-terminal group is accepted. The supplied screenshot proves that a grouping-aware real-file workflow still reaches the partial-candidate-shaped error, but it does not preserve enough boundary evidence to determine why.

The most likely uncovered boundary is the user-action contract rather than the per-edge validator: a pairwise move that joins service and admin cannot be committed as an intermediate step because the unchanged admin-to-emergency transition would remain cross-group, while strict authored-link validation makes group replacement atomic. The existing complete repair therefore depends on choosing a separate bulk create-group action and selecting all three terminals in one proposal. BUG-004's acceptance demonstrates that controlled path, but the current artifacts do not prove that the user's chosen action communicates this constraint or offers a direct resolution. A complete-candidate transport defect remains possible until the new run captures the review and serialized membership.

## Implementation

weakening validation. It retains the reviewed impact dialog, translates every
reported split edge into terminal and group names, and offers **ДОБАВИТЬ

the members of every implicated candidate group preselected and the intended
destination group name retained. The user still reviews and submits one complete
atomic candidate. When that amendment fully consumes the named destination
group, its stable group ID is reused; ordinary duplicate-name protection remains
in force for every group left outside the amendment.
