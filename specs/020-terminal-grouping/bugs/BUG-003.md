## Root Cause Analysis

The reported behavior was caused by interpreting a valid remaining-edge rejection as stale candidate validation. Source-faithful normalization maps `t-krel-service` to `singleton-7cd1dc224dc802a75a131c9b`, `t-krel-admin` to `singleton-c63fbf3079820669b21ce928`, and `t-krel-emergency` to `singleton-e422e66e867ded2106b9b34c`. The reported error places `t-krel-admin` in the service singleton (`singleton-7cd...`) and `t-krel-emergency` in its own singleton (`singleton-e422...`), proving that the candidate had already joined service to admin and that `adm-emergency` was the different edge still split by the proposed result.

BUG-002's two-terminal repair succeeds because coupling its only linked endpoints resolves every authored edge. The supplied document is a three-terminal chain. Coupling only service and admin correctly repairs `svc-access-admin` but must reject the complete candidate because `adm-emergency` remains cross-group under FR-019. Conversely, coupling only admin and emergency correctly omits `adm-emergency` from feedback but rejects `svc-access-admin`. One candidate grouping all three terminals satisfies both edges and is accepted.

The actual gap was production-fidelity coverage and diagnosis: the BUG-002 synthetic fixture did not preserve the second edge, so it could not distinguish stale validation from a different unresolved transition. Candidate membership remains exact through the Overseer, desktop facade, private contract, coordinator, session validator, and persistence boundaries.
